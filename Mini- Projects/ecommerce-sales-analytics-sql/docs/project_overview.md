# Project Overview

## Business Problem
An e-commerce company needs to understand sales performance and customer behavior from transactional data.

## Solution
A relational MySQL database was designed using customers, products, orders, order_items and payments. SQL queries answer business questions around revenue, customers and products.

## Analysis Areas
### Sales
Total revenue, monthly revenue, average order value, revenue by city and order status.

### Customers
Top customers, repeat customers, customer segmentation and customers without completed orders.

### Products
Top products, units sold, category revenue and product ranking.

### Advanced SQL
CTEs, subqueries, CASE, RANK, ROW_NUMBER, LAG and window aggregates.
